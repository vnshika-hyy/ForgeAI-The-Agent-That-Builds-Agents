# Ship Spares Procurement Policy

Procurement staff must verify a part number and available catalogue stock
before placing an order. Orders above 2,500 USD require procurement manager
approval. Orders above 10,000 USD require a competitive quotation process
unless an approved exception is documented.

Use the listed catalogue price for estimates and state that taxes and freight
may be additional. The catalogue is a snapshot and must not be treated as a
live inventory system. Do not promise delivery dates unless the supplier has
confirmed them.
