# Supplier Terms

Northstar Marine Supply normally dispatches stocked items within three business
days. Orders below 250 USD incur a 18 USD handling charge. Prices in the
catalogue are in USD and exclude applicable taxes and freight.

Harbor Technical Parts requires written confirmation for orders above 1,000
USD. Its quoted lead time for non-stock items is confirmed at order acceptance;
no fixed lead time is guaranteed in these general terms.

Bluewater Chandlery accepts returns of unopened standard items within 30 days
of delivery when accompanied by the invoice. Special-order items are
non-returnable.
