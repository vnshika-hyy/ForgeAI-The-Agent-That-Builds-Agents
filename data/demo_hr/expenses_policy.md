# Business Expenses Policy

Reasonable business travel expenses must be supported by itemized receipts.
Expense claims should be submitted within 30 calendar days of the expense.
Manager approval is required before reimbursement. Expenses above 500 USD also
require department-head approval.

Personal expenses and costs without a business purpose are not reimbursable.
This policy describes general rules and does not contain individual claim
status, payment history, or bank details.
