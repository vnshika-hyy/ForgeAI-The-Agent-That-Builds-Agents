# Annual Leave Policy

This policy applies to shore-based employees. Full-time shore employees receive
24 working days of annual leave per calendar year. Part-time entitlement is
calculated pro rata according to contracted working hours.

Leave requests should be submitted to the employee's manager at least 10
working days before the first requested day. Managers approve requests based on
operational coverage. Unused leave carry-over requires written approval from
Human Resources and must be used by 31 March of the following year.

This policy does not contain individual leave balances or employee-specific
absence records.
